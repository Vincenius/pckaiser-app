**Full description:**
PC Kaiser is a faithful mobile remake of the 1992 German strategy classic.
Take a dynasty from a single village in the year 1000 to dominion over the
whole map — by farming and trade, marriage and inheritance, espionage and
assassination, elections and open war.

- 30 realms on a freshly generated map every game
- Hot-seat multiplayer: 1–16 players on one device, AI plays the rest
- A living world: harvests, markets, plagues, earthquakes, the Reformation
  and the Ottoman invasion
- Dynasty play: marriages, births, successions, Kaiser elections with
  bribery — and epithets for the chronicle
- Espionage with genuinely hidden information: scout enemy treasuries and
  armies before you strike
- Auto-save after every turn, multiple named games, undo for mis-taps
- English and German
